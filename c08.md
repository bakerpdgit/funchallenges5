## Introduction

In this challenge, you will complete a working game of Noughts and Crosses using Python.

The game is played on a 3×3 grid. Two players take turns placing their symbols (`X` and `O`). The first player to get three of their symbols in a row, column or diagonal wins!

The program uses a **1D list** to represent the board, rather than a 2D list.

## Understanding the board

The board contains 9 positions, numbered from 0 to 8:

```text
 0 | 1 | 2
-----------
 3 | 4 | 5
-----------
 6 | 7 | 8
```

For example, the following board:

```text
 X | O | 2
-----------
 3 | X | 5
-----------
 O | 7 | X
```

would be represented by:

```python
board = ["X", "O", "", "", "X", "", "O", "", "X"]
```

An empty string (`""`) represents an unoccupied position.

*Hint:* Remember that an empty position is represented by `""`.
